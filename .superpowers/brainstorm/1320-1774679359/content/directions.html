A — The Garden

The core metaphor: money is something you grow, not just track. Nature-inspired icons, botanical language in AI responses ("growing well", "your spending is blooming"), organic card shapes. Warm and distinctly non-financial.

"Your money is a living thing. Tend to it."

B — The Journal

The core metaphor: a personal financial journal — structured, reflective, yours. Clean editorial grid, strong typographic hierarchy, understated data visualisation. The AI feels like a quiet assistant, not a chatbot. Disciplined but warm.

"A thoughtful record of your financial life."

C — The Companion

The core metaphor: a friendly, proactive companion who surfaces insights unprompted. Conversational tone, AI-first layout — the dashboard leads with an observation, not just a number. Warm but risks feeling patronising if the copy isn't perfect.

"Less dashboard. More dialogue."

B is my recommendation — but tell me where I'm wrong.
