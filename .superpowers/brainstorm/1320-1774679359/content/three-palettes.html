MY PICK

Coral wins on warmth

Most aligned with "friendly, funny, non-judgmental." Light base means easy glancing outdoors. Warm accent matches CAIT's personality. Closest to the brand voice you described.

MOST DISTINCTIVE

Teal wins on uniqueness

Nobody in UK fintech uses this colour. That's a real advantage on the App Store. Slightly cooler personality — but still warm enough. Grows on you.

MOST PREMIUM

Brilliant wins on impact

Highest visual drama. Looks stunning in screenshots. But dark apps feel more like tools than companions — which cuts against CAIT's "friendly inner voice" concept.
